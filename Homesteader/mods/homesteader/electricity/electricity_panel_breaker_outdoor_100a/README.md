# Outdoor Breaker Box 100A

**Item ID:** `electricity_panel_breaker_outdoor_100a`  
**Category:** `electricity`  
**Icon:** ![Outdoor Breaker Box 100A](electricity_panel_breaker_outdoor_100a.png)

A weatherproof 100 amp service breaker box. Mount it on the OUTSIDE of a building (left click a wall with it in hand) to bring electricity to that building: its lights then work.
