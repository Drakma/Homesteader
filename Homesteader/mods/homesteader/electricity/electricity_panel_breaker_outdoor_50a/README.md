# Outdoor Breaker Box 50A

**Item ID:** `electricity_panel_breaker_outdoor_50a`  
**Category:** `electricity`  
**Icon:** ![Outdoor Breaker Box 50A](electricity_panel_breaker_outdoor_50a.png)

A weatherproof 50 amp service breaker box. Mount it on the OUTSIDE of a building (left click a wall with it in hand) to bring electricity to that building: its lights then work.
