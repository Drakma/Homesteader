# DC Bus Bar, Small

**Item ID:** `electricity_busbar_dc_100a`  
**Category:** `electricity`  
**Icon:** ![DC Bus Bar, Small](electricity_busbar_dc_100a.png)

A 100 amp DC bus bar: a positive and a negative copper bar with 6 studs each on an insulating plate, to join the solar, battery and inverter wiring in one place. It mounts on a building's wall: aim at a wall of a finished building and left click.
