# Wall Wire Entry

**Item ID:** `electricity_wall_entry_wire`  
**Category:** `electricity`  
**Icon:** ![Wall Wire Entry](electricity_wall_entry_wire.png)

A weatherproof wire entry that goes THROUGH a wall, to bring wires into a building from outside. Aim at a wall of a finished building and left click: it shows on both sides (a plate with a sealed cable gland on each, joined by a sleeve through the wall) and can be clicked from either side.
