# MPPT Charge Controller, Medium

**Item ID:** `electricity_controller_mppt_64a`  
**Category:** `electricity`  
**Icon:** ![MPPT Charge Controller, Medium](electricity_controller_mppt_64a.png)

A 64 amp MPPT solar charge controller (12/24/48 volt battery systems, up to 150 volts of solar, about 3.0 kW at 48 volts) with a finned heat sink, an LCD readout and a terminal row. It mounts on a building's wall: aim at a wall of a finished building and left click.
