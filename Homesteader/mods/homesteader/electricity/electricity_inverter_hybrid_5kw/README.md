# Hybrid Inverter, Medium

**Item ID:** `electricity_inverter_hybrid_5kw`  
**Category:** `electricity`  
**Icon:** ![Hybrid Inverter, Medium](electricity_inverter_hybrid_5kw.png)

A 5 kW hybrid inverter: it turns the battery bank's direct current into household power, takes solar and the grid or a generator, and charges the batteries. It has a finned heat sink, a fan grille, an LCD readout and a DC isolator. It mounts on a building's wall: aim at a wall of a finished building and left click.
