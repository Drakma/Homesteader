# MPPT Charge Controller, Small

**Item ID:** `electricity_controller_mppt_32a`  
**Category:** `electricity`  
**Icon:** ![MPPT Charge Controller, Small](electricity_controller_mppt_32a.png)

A 32 amp MPPT solar charge controller (12/24/48 volt battery systems, up to 150 volts of solar, about 1.5 kW at 48 volts) with a finned heat sink, an LCD readout and a terminal row. It mounts on a building's wall: aim at a wall of a finished building and left click.
