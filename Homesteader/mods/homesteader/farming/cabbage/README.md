# Cabbage Plant (`cabbage`)

![Cabbage Plant Icon](cabbage.png)

## Overview
- **Category**: `farming`
- **Item Identifier**: `cabbage`

## Description
A cabbage lifecycle growing through 5 stages (sprout -> small -> medium -> mature -> ripe) over 80 days, with withered and dead versions. The models are procedural (built in the game), the originals are kept in assets/_originals/crops_old.

## Growth
- **Type**: `crop`
- **Species**: `cabbage`
- **Full age**: 80 days
- **Yield**: 1 to 2 `food_cabbage` at stage 5
- **Planting**: spring, fall; seed `seed_cabbage`

| Stage | Name | Age range |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | sprout | 0 to 9 |
| 2 | small | 9 to 26 |
| 3 | medium | 26 to 48 |
| 4 | mature | 48 to 66 |
| 5 | ripe | 66 to 80 |
