# Peas Plant (`peas`)

![Peas Plant Icon](peas.png)

## Overview
- **Category**: `farming`
- **Item Identifier**: `peas`

## Description
A peas lifecycle growing through 5 stages (sprout -> small -> medium -> mature -> ripe) over 60 days, with withered and dead versions. The models are procedural (built in the game), the originals are kept in assets/_originals/crops_old.

## Growth
- **Type**: `crop`
- **Species**: `peas`
- **Full age**: 60 days
- **Yield**: 3 to 6 `food_peas` at stage 5
- **Planting**: spring, fall; seed `seed_peas`

| Stage | Name | Age range |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | sprout | 0 to 7 |
| 2 | small | 7 to 20 |
| 3 | medium | 20 to 36 |
| 4 | mature | 36 to 50 |
| 5 | ripe | 50 to 60 |
