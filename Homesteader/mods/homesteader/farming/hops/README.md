# Hops Plant (`hops`)

![Hops Plant Icon](hops.png)

## Overview
- **Category**: `farming`
- **Item Identifier**: `hops`

## Description
A hops lifecycle growing through 5 stages (sprout -> small -> medium -> mature -> ripe) over 120 days, with withered and dead versions. The models are procedural (built in the game), the originals are kept in assets/_originals/crops_old.

## Growth
- **Type**: `crop`
- **Species**: `hops`
- **Full age**: 120 days
- **Yield**: 2 to 2 `ingredient_hops` at stage 5
- **Planting**: summer; seed `seed_hops`

| Stage | Name | Age range |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | sprout | 0 to 13 |
| 2 | small | 13 to 40 |
| 3 | medium | 40 to 72 |
| 4 | mature | 72 to 100 |
| 5 | ripe | 100 to 120 |
