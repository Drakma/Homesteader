# Peanut Plant (`peanuts`)

![Peanut Plant Icon](peanuts.png)

## Overview
- **Category**: `farming`
- **Item Identifier**: `peanuts`

## Description
A peanut lifecycle growing through 5 stages (sprout -> small -> medium -> mature -> ripe) over 110 days, with withered and dead versions. The models are procedural (built in the game), the originals are kept in assets/_originals/crops_old.

## Growth
- **Type**: `crop`
- **Species**: `peanuts`
- **Full age**: 110 days
- **Yield**: 3 to 6 `food_peanuts` at stage 5
- **Planting**: summer; seed `seed_peanuts`

| Stage | Name | Age range |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | sprout | 0 to 12 |
| 2 | small | 12 to 36 |
| 3 | medium | 36 to 66 |
| 4 | mature | 66 to 91 |
| 5 | ripe | 91 to 110 |
