# Carrot Plant (`carrot`)

![Carrot Plant Icon](carrot.png)

## Overview
- **Category**: `farming`
- **Item Identifier**: `carrot`

## Description
A carrot lifecycle growing through 5 stages (sprout -> small -> medium -> mature -> ripe) over 70 days, with withered and dead versions. The models are procedural (built in the game), the originals are kept in assets/_originals/crops_old.

## Growth
- **Type**: `crop`
- **Species**: `carrot`
- **Full age**: 70 days
- **Yield**: 2 to 4 `food_carrot` at stage 5
- **Planting**: spring; seed `seed_carrot`

| Stage | Name | Age range |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | sprout | 0 to 8 |
| 2 | small | 8 to 23 |
| 3 | medium | 23 to 42 |
| 4 | mature | 42 to 58 |
| 5 | ripe | 58 to 70 |
