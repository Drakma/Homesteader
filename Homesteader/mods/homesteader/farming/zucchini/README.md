# Zucchini Plant (`zucchini`)

![Zucchini Plant Icon](zucchini.png)

## Overview
- **Category**: `farming`
- **Item Identifier**: `zucchini`

## Description
A zucchini lifecycle growing through 5 stages (sprout -> small -> medium -> mature -> ripe) over 50 days, with withered and dead versions. The models are procedural (built in the game), the originals are kept in assets/_originals/crops_old.

## Growth
- **Type**: `crop`
- **Species**: `zucchini`
- **Full age**: 50 days
- **Yield**: 2 to 4 `food_zucchini` at stage 5
- **Planting**: spring, summer; seed `seed_zucchini`

| Stage | Name | Age range |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | sprout | 0 to 6 |
| 2 | small | 6 to 16 |
| 3 | medium | 16 to 30 |
| 4 | mature | 30 to 42 |
| 5 | ripe | 42 to 50 |
