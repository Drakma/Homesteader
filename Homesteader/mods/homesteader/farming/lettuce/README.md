# Lettuce Plant (`lettuce`)

![Lettuce Plant Icon](lettuce.png)

## Overview
- **Category**: `farming`
- **Item Identifier**: `lettuce`

## Description
A lettuce lifecycle growing through 5 stages (sprout -> small -> medium -> mature -> ripe) over 45 days, with withered and dead versions. The models are procedural (built in the game), the originals are kept in assets/_originals/crops_old.

## Growth
- **Type**: `crop`
- **Species**: `lettuce`
- **Full age**: 45 days
- **Yield**: 1 to 2 `food_lettuce` at stage 5
- **Planting**: spring, fall; seed `seed_lettuce`

| Stage | Name | Age range |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | sprout | 0 to 5 |
| 2 | small | 5 to 15 |
| 3 | medium | 15 to 27 |
| 4 | mature | 27 to 37 |
| 5 | ripe | 37 to 45 |
