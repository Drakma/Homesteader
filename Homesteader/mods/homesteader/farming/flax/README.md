# Flax Plant (`flax`)

![Flax Plant Icon](flax.png)

## Overview
- **Category**: `farming`
- **Item Identifier**: `flax`

## Description
A flax lifecycle growing through 5 stages (sprout -> small -> medium -> mature -> ripe) over 100 days, with withered and dead versions. The models are procedural (built in the game), the originals are kept in assets/_originals/crops_old.

## Growth
- **Type**: `crop`
- **Species**: `flax`
- **Full age**: 100 days
- **Yield**: 2 to 2 `ingredient_flax` at stage 5
- **Planting**: spring; seed `seed_flax`

| Stage | Name | Age range |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | sprout | 0 to 11 |
| 2 | small | 11 to 33 |
| 3 | medium | 33 to 60 |
| 4 | mature | 60 to 83 |
| 5 | ripe | 83 to 100 |
