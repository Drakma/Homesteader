# Straw Bedding

Loose straw spread over the floor of a room as animal bedding. Made by spreading a straw bale: hold a bale and right click the floor of a room in a building. A shovel takes it up again.

Procedural model (tools/make_straw_bedding.py), 646 triangles: a heap of loose straw, used for the icon. In the game the bedding is laid as one mesh over the floor of the room (`Assets/Scripts/Growth/StrawBedding.cs`); one bale covers 12 square metres.
