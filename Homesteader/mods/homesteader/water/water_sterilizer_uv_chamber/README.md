# UV Sterilizer Chamber

A UV steriliser: water runs through a stainless chamber past an ultraviolet lamp, the last stage for drinking water. Mount it on a wall and pipe the line through it.

Procedural model (tools/make_water_filters.py), 444 triangles, 0.58 x 0.12 x 0.30 m. A stainless chamber along the wall in two clips, a port at each end, the lamp cap and its lead to a ballast box. Mounts on a wall, either side; `wire_role: water_filter`. See docs/WELLS.md.
