# 3/4 HP Submersible Well Pump

**Item ID:** `water_pump_submersible_three_quarter_hp`  
**Category:** `water`  
**Icon:** ![3/4 HP Submersible Well Pump](water_pump_submersible_three_quarter_hp.png)

A 3/4 HP stainless submersible well pump: it lifts up to 2300 liters an hour (the well's own yield may be less). It goes down a drilled well when the pump stage is built, and runs on the well's sub panel (1200 W).
