# Whole-House Reverse Osmosis System

A floor-standing whole-house reverse osmosis system: a booster pump, three pre-filters and two membrane housings on a steel frame. It makes about 3,000 gallons (11,000 L) of clean water a day, and rejects about a liter to the drain for every liter it makes. Pipe the line through it.

Procedural model (tools/make_water_filters_large.py), 804 triangles, 0.76 x 0.68 x 1.23 m. Stands on the floor; `wire_role: water_filter`, 470 L/h, recovery 50 %. See docs/WATER_FLOW.md.
