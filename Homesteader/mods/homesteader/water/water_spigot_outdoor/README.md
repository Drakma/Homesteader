# Outdoor Spigot

A brass hose bib on a wall plate. Mount it on the outside of a building that has water; a right click with a bucket, jug or watering can in hand fills it. It gives whatever water the building is supplied with, raw or filtered.

Procedural model (tools/make_water_fixtures.py), 160 triangles. Mounts on a wall (exterior side); `wire_role: water_spigot`. See docs/WELLS.md.
