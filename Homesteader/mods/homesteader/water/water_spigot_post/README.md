# Spigot Post

A brass hose bib on a wooden post, for water out in the yard. Set it in the ground and pipe it to a water tank, a filter or a well (pipe wrench, PVC pipe); a right click with a bucket, jug or watering can in hand then fills it. It gives whatever water it is piped to, raw or filtered.

Procedural model (tools/make_spigot_post.py), 910 triangles, 0.68 x 0.68 x 1.00 m. `wire_role: water_spigot`, standing in the yard (not mounted): it is piped. See docs/WELLS.md.
