# Wall Rack

An eight unit rack that bolts to a wall. Rackmount routers and switches slide into it (left click with one in hand) and are cabled as one.

Procedural model (tools/make_internet.py): an eight unit open wall rack. Its detail is painted (tools/net_pictures.py). 94 triangles, 0.53 x 0.42 x 0.42 m.
