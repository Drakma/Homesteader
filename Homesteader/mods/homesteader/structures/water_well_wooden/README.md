# Drilled Well (Wooden Well House)

**Item ID:** `water_well_wooden`  
**Category:** `structures`  
**Icon:** ![Drilled Well (Wooden Well House)](water_well_wooden.png)

An electric drilled well: a submersible pump down the bore, a PVC drop pipe bringing the water up and a small wooden well house over the head. Fit a well sub panel on its post and wire that to a breaker box with AC wire to run the pump; pipe it to a tank with PVC pipe. The pump stage asks for pipe and cable by the well's depth (hover the blueprint over a spot to see the depth and the most it can give).
