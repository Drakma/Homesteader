# Building Waste Outlet

**Item ID:** `waste_outlet_house`  
**Category:** `waste`  
**Icon:** ![Building Waste Outlet](waste_outlet_house.png)

Takes a building's waste water out: a black drain pipe through the wall with a cleanout cap, going down into the ground. Mount it on the OUTSIDE of a building (left click a wall with it in hand); it shows on the outside only. Then pipe it to a septic tank with black waste pipe and a pipe wrench (a macerator pump inside the building on the way is optional). The sinks, toilets, showers and washers in the building drain through it.

Procedural model (tools/make_septic.py). See docs/SEPTIC.md.
