# Bulk Diesel

100 L of diesel fuel, delivered. The fuel truck brings it the morning after you order and pumps it into your tanks, one after another: every tank that takes it and is empty or already holds it. What fits in no tank is refunded.

No model: a lot of 100 L of `liquid_diesel` (tools/make_bulk_fuel.py). Delivered by the fuel truck: `Assets/Scripts/World/FuelDelivery.cs`.
