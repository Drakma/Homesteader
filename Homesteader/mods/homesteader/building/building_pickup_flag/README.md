# Pickup Flag

A flag on a steel pole. Plant it in the yard: it marks the corner of your market pickup zone, a 10 by 10 tile square reaching from the flag toward the road. Goods left inside it are what the Market app sells and where deliveries are dropped.

Procedural model (tools/make_pickup_flag.py), 164 triangles, 1.12 x 0.30 x 2.47 m.
