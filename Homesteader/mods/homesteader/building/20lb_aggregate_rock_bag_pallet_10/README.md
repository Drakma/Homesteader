# Bag of Aggregate Rock Pallet

A wooden pallet stacked with 10 of: a small 20 lb bag of aggregate rock in a stone colored bag with black accents.

Procedural model (tools/make_bag_pallets.py) from `20lb_aggregate_rock_bag`: 10 bags in 2 layers on a 1.2 m by 1.0 m pallet, 276 triangles, 0.34 m tall.
