fix(assets/hud_kit/font): correct sxHeight math

Previously wrote sxHeight = int(UPEM * 10 * 0.625) = 6400. The
intended value is int(10 * PX) = 640 (10 design pixels converted
to font units). This was a typo -- the multiplication by UPEM
should not have happened.

sxHeight is what UI Toolkit uses to compute the lowercase baseline
of monospaced fonts; an 8x overshoot silently drags glyphs toward
the descender area.

All five TTFs rebuilt with the corrected math and re-emitted.
Tools/bake_kit_fonts.py propagates the rebuild.
