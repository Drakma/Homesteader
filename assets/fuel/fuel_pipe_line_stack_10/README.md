# Fuel Line Stack (`fuel_pipe_line_stack_10`)

10 lengths of fuel line strapped together, 30 m in all: gasoline and diesel line for a long run to a pump.

Procedural model (tools/make_fuel_line_stacks.py): 10 lengths in 4 rows, strapped, 1356 triangles. `wire_kind: fuel`, 30 m a stack. See docs/WIRING.md.
