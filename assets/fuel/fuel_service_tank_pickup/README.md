# Tank Pickup

The fuel truck comes the next morning and takes one of your big fuel tanks away (a tank is too heavy to pick up). It takes your emptiest tank; order one pickup for each tank. Fuel still in the tank goes with it. Half the price of the tank is credited to you.

No model: a service (tools/make_bulk_fuel.py). `Assets/Scripts/World/FuelDelivery.cs`.
