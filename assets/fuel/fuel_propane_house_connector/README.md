# Propane House Connector

The connection that brings propane into a building: a shut-off valve with a yellow lever and a regulator on a wall bracket. Mount it on the OUTSIDE of a building (left click a wall with it in hand), then pipe it to a propane tank with propane pipe and a pipe wrench.

Procedural model (tools/make_propane_line.py), 586 triangles, 0.20 x 0.12 x 0.46 m. Mounts on a wall (exterior side); `wire_role: propane_connector`. See docs/WIRING.md.
