# DC Circuit Breaker

A white DIN-rail DC miniature circuit breaker (MCB) that protects a battery or solar circuit, two poles.

Procedural model (tools/make_breakers.py), 420 triangles, 0.036 x 0.087 x 0.084 m. Rated 64 A (2 poles): the rating is printed on the front and stored in `category_fields.max_current_a`.
