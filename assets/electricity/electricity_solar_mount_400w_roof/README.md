# 400W Solar Roof Mount

**Item ID:** `electricity_solar_mount_400w_roof`  
**Category:** `electricity`  
**Icon:** ![400W Solar Roof Mount](electricity_solar_mount_400w_roof.png)

A steel roof mount for the 400W Commercial Solar Panel. Put it on a roof: aim at the roof from the ground (there are no ladders) and left click, then fit the panel: right click the mount with the panel in hand.
