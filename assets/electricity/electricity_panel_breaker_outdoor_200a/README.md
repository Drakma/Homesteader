# Outdoor Breaker Box 200A

**Item ID:** `electricity_panel_breaker_outdoor_200a`  
**Category:** `electricity`  
**Icon:** ![Outdoor Breaker Box 200A](electricity_panel_breaker_outdoor_200a.png)

A weatherproof 200 amp service breaker box. Mount it on the OUTSIDE of a building (left click a wall with it in hand) to bring electricity to that building: its lights then work.
