# Fieldstone Chimney Kit

A pallet of split fieldstone, a steel flue liner and a bag of mortar mix: everything for a stone chimney with a fireplace. Hold it, click an outside wall of a finished building (from outside or from inside) and click again to build it: the stack stands outside and rises a metre past the roof, the mantel and firebox are inside. It cannot go on a window or a door.

Procedural model (tools/make_chimney_kits.py), 676 triangles, 1.00 x 0.81 x 0.66 m.
