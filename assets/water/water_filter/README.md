# Water Filter

A cartridge sediment filter on a wall bracket: a first, coarse stage for a water line. Mount it on a wall and pipe the line through it.

Procedural model (tools/make_water_filters.py), 840 triangles, 0.21 x 0.12 x 0.34 m. A black head with two ports under a wall bracket, a ribbed blue sump. Mounts on a wall, either side; `wire_role: water_filter`. See docs/WELLS.md.
