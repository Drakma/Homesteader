# Reverse Osmosis Filter

A multi-stage reverse osmosis drinking water system. Mount it on a wall and pipe the line through it.

Procedural model (tools/make_water_filters.py), 2224 triangles, 0.42 x 0.13 x 0.49 m. Three cartridge sumps under a white bracket, the membrane housing and a post filter across the top, a gauge and coloured tubes. Mounts on a wall, either side; `wire_role: water_filter`. See docs/WELLS.md.
