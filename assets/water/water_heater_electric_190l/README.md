# Electric Water Heater

**Item ID:** `water_heater_electric_190l`  
**Category:** `water`  
**Icon:** ![Electric Water Heater](water_heater_electric_190l.png)

A 190 liter (50 gallon) electric water heater with a 4500 W element. Pipe it after the filters (pipe wrench, PVC pipe): it fills like a tank and heats what it holds to 55 C while the building it stands in has power, drawing its element's watts while it heats.
