# 1 HP Submersible Well Pump

**Item ID:** `water_pump_submersible_1hp`  
**Category:** `water`  
**Icon:** ![1 HP Submersible Well Pump](water_pump_submersible_1hp.png)

A 1 HP stainless submersible well pump: it lifts up to 2900 liters an hour (the well's own yield may be less). It goes down a drilled well when the pump stage is built, and runs on the well's sub panel (1500 W).
