# Utility Faucet

A chrome utility faucet with hot and cold levers. Mount it inside a building that has water; a right click with a bucket, jug or watering can in hand fills it with whatever water the building is supplied with: raw unless it comes through the sediment, carbon and UV filters.

Procedural model (tools/make_water_fixtures.py), 144 triangles. Mounts on a wall (interior side); `wire_role: water_spigot`. See docs/WELLS.md.
