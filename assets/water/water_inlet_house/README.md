# Building Water Inlet

Brings water into a building: a ball valve with a blue lever on a PVC riser, on a wall bracket. Mount it on the OUTSIDE of a building (left click a wall with it in hand): it goes THROUGH the wall, with a valve on the inside too. Then pipe it to a tank, a filter or a well with PVC pipe and a pipe wrench. The building then has water at its spigots and faucets.

Procedural model (tools/make_water_fixtures.py), 284 triangles. Mounts on a wall (exterior side); `wire_role: water_inlet`. See docs/WELLS.md.
