# Radish Plant (`radish`)

![Radish Plant Icon](radish.png)

## Overview
- **Category**: `farming`
- **Item Identifier**: `radish`

## Description
A radish lifecycle growing through 5 stages (sprout -> small -> medium -> mature -> ripe) over 30 days, with withered and dead versions. The models are procedural (built in the game), the originals are kept in assets/_originals/crops_old.

## Growth
- **Type**: `crop`
- **Species**: `radish`
- **Full age**: 30 days
- **Yield**: 3 to 6 `food_radish` at stage 5
- **Planting**: spring, fall; seed `seed_radish`

| Stage | Name | Age range |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | sprout | 0 to 3 |
| 2 | small | 3 to 10 |
| 3 | medium | 10 to 18 |
| 4 | mature | 18 to 25 |
| 5 | ripe | 25 to 30 |
