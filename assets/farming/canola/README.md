# Canola Plant (`canola`)

![Canola Plant Icon](canola.png)

## Overview
- **Category**: `farming`
- **Item Identifier**: `canola`

## Description
A canola lifecycle growing through 5 stages (sprout -> small -> medium -> mature -> ripe) over 95 days, with withered and dead versions. The models are procedural (built in the game), the originals are kept in assets/_originals/crops_old.

## Growth
- **Type**: `crop`
- **Species**: `canola`
- **Full age**: 95 days
- **Yield**: 2 to 2 `ingredient_canola` at stage 5
- **Planting**: spring; seed `seed_canola`

| Stage | Name | Age range |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | sprout | 0 to 10 |
| 2 | small | 10 to 31 |
| 3 | medium | 31 to 57 |
| 4 | mature | 57 to 79 |
| 5 | ripe | 79 to 95 |
