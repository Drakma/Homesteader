# Tomato Plant (`tomato`)

![Tomato Plant Icon](tomato.png)

## Overview
- **Category**: `farming`
- **Item Identifier**: `tomato`

## Description
A tomato lifecycle growing through 5 stages (sprout -> small -> medium -> mature -> ripe) over 75 days, with withered and dead versions. The models are procedural (built in the game), the originals are kept in assets/_originals/crops_old.

## Growth
- **Type**: `crop`
- **Species**: `tomato`
- **Full age**: 75 days
- **Yield**: 3 to 6 `food_tomato` at stage 5
- **Planting**: spring, summer; seed `seed_tomato`

| Stage | Name | Age range |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | sprout | 0 to 8 |
| 2 | small | 8 to 25 |
| 3 | medium | 25 to 45 |
| 4 | mature | 45 to 62 |
| 5 | ripe | 62 to 75 |
