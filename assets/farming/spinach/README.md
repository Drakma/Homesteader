# Spinach Plant (`spinach`)

![Spinach Plant Icon](spinach.png)

## Overview
- **Category**: `farming`
- **Item Identifier**: `spinach`

## Description
A spinach lifecycle growing through 5 stages (sprout -> small -> medium -> mature -> ripe) over 40 days, with withered and dead versions. The models are procedural (built in the game), the originals are kept in assets/_originals/crops_old.

## Growth
- **Type**: `crop`
- **Species**: `spinach`
- **Full age**: 40 days
- **Yield**: 2 to 4 `food_spinach` at stage 5
- **Planting**: spring, fall; seed `seed_spinach`

| Stage | Name | Age range |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | sprout | 0 to 4 |
| 2 | small | 4 to 13 |
| 3 | medium | 13 to 24 |
| 4 | mature | 24 to 33 |
| 5 | ripe | 33 to 40 |
