# Cucumber Plant (`cucumber`)

![Cucumber Plant Icon](cucumber.png)

## Overview
- **Category**: `farming`
- **Item Identifier**: `cucumber`

## Description
A cucumber lifecycle growing through 5 stages (sprout -> small -> medium -> mature -> ripe) over 55 days, with withered and dead versions. The models are procedural (built in the game), the originals are kept in assets/_originals/crops_old.

## Growth
- **Type**: `crop`
- **Species**: `cucumber`
- **Full age**: 55 days
- **Yield**: 2 to 4 `food_cucumber` at stage 5
- **Planting**: spring, summer; seed `seed_cucumber`

| Stage | Name | Age range |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | sprout | 0 to 6 |
| 2 | small | 6 to 18 |
| 3 | medium | 18 to 33 |
| 4 | mature | 33 to 46 |
| 5 | ripe | 46 to 55 |
